Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Simon Fresh Water ("we", "us", "our") respects the privacy of our customers and is committed to protecting their personal data in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we safeguard it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Identity & Contact Data: full name, account name, meter account number, phone number, email address.
  • Location Data: physical address and, where provided, GPS coordinates of the water meter or residence.
  • Usage & Billing Data: meter readings (M³), consumption, water bills, payment records (including M-Pesa transaction references).
  • Communication Data: SMS and WhatsApp message history sent to the customer for billing and reminder purposes.

2. Why We Collect It

  • To prepare accurate water bills based on meter readings.
  • To deliver bills, reminders, and payment confirmations by SMS, WhatsApp, or email.
  • To reconcile M-Pesa payments received to the correct account.
  • To maintain service records and respond to customer enquiries.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations in Kenya.

3. Lawful Basis

We process personal data on the basis of:

  • Contract: to provide water utility services to the consumer.
  • Legal obligation: to comply with tax, utility, and data protection laws.
  • Legitimate interest: to send billing reminders and prevent fraud.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may share data with:

  • Safaricom Plc — for M-Pesa payment processing and reconciliation.
  • Meta Platforms — where WhatsApp reminders are sent to the consumer's phone number.
  • SMSGateway providers — to deliver SMS messages.
  • Government authorities — only when legally required.

5. Data Retention

Consumer records, meter readings, and payment history are retained for as long as the account remains active and for a minimum of seven (7) years thereafter, as required for tax and accounting purposes. Terminated accounts are retained in an inactive state until deleted by the customer or by the administrator.

6. Data Security

All data is stored on encrypted servers. Access to the billing system is restricted to authorised administrators using password protection and, where enabled, SMS-based two-factor authentication. Data in transit is protected using HTTPS.

7. Your Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Object to processing or withdraw consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (ODPC).

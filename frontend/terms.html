3. Billing Accuracy

  • Water bills are calculated as (current meter reading − previous meter reading) × KES 90 per M³, in accordance with consumption-based billing principles.
  • Payments are applied using the FIFO (First In, First Out) method — the oldest unpaid bill is cleared first.
  • Any payment exceeding the total outstanding is treated as prepayment credit and applied to future bills.
  • Consumers may dispute any bill within 30 days of receiving it.

4. M-Pesa Payments

  • Payments made via M-Pesa (Paybill 247247) are automatically reconciled against the consumer's account using the meter number as the account reference.
  • Where the account reference is incorrect, the payment may be held for manual review and assigned by an administrator.
  • Safaricom's standard M-Pesa transaction charges apply to the payer and are not the responsibility of Simon Fresh Water.

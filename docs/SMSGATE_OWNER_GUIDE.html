SIMON FRESH WATER — SMS Gateway Setup

One-time setup guide for the business owner · Kikuyu, Kenya

Simon Fresh Water sends automatic SMS messages to consumers — bill reminders, payment confirmations, and admin login codes. These messages go out through your own phone using the free SMSGate app. Once set up, your phone becomes the business SMS gateway: every consumer sees the business number as the sender, and you control the SMS airtime yourself.

1 What You Need

  • An Android phone — any model, old or new. It will stay at the office, plugged in and switched on, 24/7.
  • A dedicated SIM card — ideally the business number you want consumers to see (e.g. 0791 XXX XXX). This SIM must not be registered on WhatsApp.
  • Airtime on the SIM — start with KES 500 and top up monthly. Each SMS costs roughly KES 1–2.
  • Wi-Fi or mobile data — for the SMSGate app to talk to the cloud server.
  • 5 minutes of your time.
💡 Tip: Use a cheap Android phone dedicated to this task, not your daily phone. That way you can leave it at the office permanently without disrupting your day.

2 Install the SMSGate App

  1. On the dedicated Android phone, open the browser and go to:
    https://github.com/capcom6/android-sms-gateway/releases
  2. Download the latest release APK (file ending in .apk).
  3. Tap the downloaded file → allow "Install from unknown sources" if asked.
  4. Open the SMSGate app after install.
  5. When the app asks for permissions, tap Allow for:
    • SEND_SMS — required to actually send messages
    • READ_PHONE_STATE — required to know the SIM status
    • READ_SMS and RECEIVE_SMS — optional but recommended

3 Enable Cloud Server Mode

  1. On the SMSGate home screen, tap Cloud Server (or switch to it).
  2. Wait a few seconds — the app auto-registers with the Simon Fresh Water account.
  3. The app will display three important values. Write them down exactly in the spaces provided below:
Username
Password
Device ID
SIM Phone Number
If the app does not show credentials: tap ☰ Menu → Settings → Cloud Server. The credentials are shown there.

4 Send the Credentials to Your Developer

Copy the four values from Step 3 into a WhatsApp message or email to your developer:

  • Username
  • Password
  • Device ID
  • SIM Phone Number

The developer will update the server with these values. From that moment, every SMS sent by Simon Fresh Water goes out from your phone and appears to consumers as coming from your business number.

⚠️ Important: Never share these credentials with anyone except your developer. Anyone with these credentials can send SMS through your phone.

5 Optimise Your Phone for Reliability

The phone must stay responsive at all times. Complete these settings:

5a. Battery optimisation

  1. Open Settings on the phone → Apps → SMSGate.
  2. Tap Battery → select Unrestricted (or Don't optimise).
  3. On some phones: Settings → Battery → Battery optimisation → find SMSGate → Don't optimise.

5b. Auto-start on boot

  1. Inside the SMSGate app → Settings → System.
  2. Enable Start on boot.
  3. Enable Foreground service (keeps the app running even when the screen is off).

5c. Physical setup

  • Keep the phone plugged into a charger at all times.
  • Place it in a dry, ventilated spot at the office.
  • Keep it connected to Wi-Fi (or leave mobile data on).
  • Do not turn the phone off, restart it, or let the battery drain.

6 Monthly SIM Top-Up

Every month, top up the SIM with enough airtime for the SMS volume. Estimate:

  • 20 consumers × 2 reminders = 40 SMS ≈ KES 80/month
  • 100 consumers × 2 reminders = 200 SMS ≈ KES 400/month
  • 500 consumers × 2 reminders = 1,000 SMS ≈ KES 2,000/month

Use M-Pesa to top up the SIM directly. Set a monthly reminder on your main phone.

✅ You're done! Once the developer confirms setup, your phone will silently send every SMS that Simon Fresh Water needs.

7 What to Do If Something Goes Wrong

SMS not being delivered

  1. Check the SIM has airtime (dial *334# on that phone).
  2. Check the phone has network signal.
  3. Check the phone has internet (Wi-Fi or data).
  4. Open the SMSGate app → Messages tab → look for the queued messages and their status.

App keeps stopping

  1. Confirm battery optimisation is off (see Step 5a).
  2. Open the app once a week — sometimes Android kills background apps that aren't opened for a long time.

I lost the phone

  1. Tell your developer immediately.
  2. They will temporarily disable SMS from the server.
  3. Set up SMSGate on a new phone following this guide from Step 1.

I forgot the credentials

  1. Open the SMSGate app → Settings → Cloud Server.
  2. The credentials are always visible in the app.

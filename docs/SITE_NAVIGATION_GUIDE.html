Simon Fresh Water — Admin Billing Portal

1 🔐

Signing In

From the home page, click the Login button in the header (or navigate to /admin/login).

  • Enter your username and password, then tap Sign In.
  • If two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled, a 6-digit code is sent by SMS to your registered phone. Enter the code on the next screen.
  • After successful verification you are redirected back to the home page, now fully authenticated as an administrator.
Tip: Forgot your password? Use the Forgot password? link on the login page. A reset code will be sent to your registered phone.
2 🏠

Home Page — Search & Lookup

The home page is your starting point for account operations. The search bar accepts either:

  • A customer name (e.g. "John Kamau")
  • A meter account number (e.g. "MTR-0012")

Results appear as clickable cards showing the customer's name, meter number, and current bill status. Tap any result to open the full consumer page.

Note: Searching requires you to be logged in. If you're signed out, a polite prompt asks you to log in first.
3 ➕

New Customer

Purpose: Register a new water consumer and open their billing account.

Click the ➕ New Customer button in the header. A form opens, and you fill in the consumer's details:

Customer NameFull name of the consumer
Account NameHousehold / business name
Meter Acc. No.Unique meter account code
Initial ReadingMeter's starting value (M³)
ContactPhone number for SMS & WhatsApp
EmailOptional email address
AddressPhysical location text
Latitude & LongitudeOptional GPS coordinates

✓ Consumer Consent

Before saving, confirm that the consumer has given consent for their personal data (name, phone number, email, address, meter readings) to be stored and used for water utility billing purposes. This consent is required under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019. Consumers will receive SMS/WhatsApp messages for bills, payment reminders, and account notifications.

Tip: If the meter starts at zero (brand-new installation), leave the Initial Reading as 0. If the meter already had a value, enter that value so the first bill reflects only the usage since registration.
4 ⛔

Terminate Customer

Purpose: Deactivate a consumer's account while preserving all historical data (readings, payments, statements).

Click the ⛔ Terminate button in the header. A search box appears — find the consumer by name or meter number, then tap Terminate.

  • Terminated consumers are marked TERMINATED in red and can no longer submit meter readings or receive SMS reminders.
  • Their account page shows two options at the bottom:
    • ♻️ Reactivate — restores the account to active status.
    • 🗑️ Permanently Delete — erases the customer and all related data (irreversible).
Note: Termination is a soft-delete — nothing is lost. Only the permanent delete option removes data from the system.
5 👤

Consumer Page

Tap any search result to open the consumer's account page. Everything you need for one consumer is on this page:

  • Overview card — customer name, account name, meter number, contact, email, address, and current bill status (Cleared Due Overdue Prepayment).
  • ✏️ Edit button — inline-edit any detail and save changes.
  • 📊 Download Statement — full accounting ledger PDF.
  • Send Bill Reminder — send an SMS or WhatsApp message with the current outstanding balance.
  • Submit New Meter Reading — record the current cumulative reading (in M³). The bill is calculated automatically as (current − previous) × KES 90.
  • 💰 Record Payment — record a manual payment. Payments are applied to the oldest unpaid bill first (FIFO); any excess becomes prepayment credit.
  • Last 5 Meter Readings — clickable table; each reading links to its individual bill.
  • 📈 Consumption Trend — visual chart of the last 12 readings.
6 🧾

Water Bill

Click any row in the readings table to view the individual bill for that specific meter reading. The bill shows:

  • Reading date and cumulative meter value
  • Consumption for that reading (current − previous)
  • Bill amount (consumption × KES 90)
  • Amount paid and remaining balance
  • Current status of that specific bill

Use the 📄 Download Water Bill (PDF) button at the bottom to save or print a formal bill.

7 📲

M-Pesa Dashboard

Purpose: Automatically reconcile consumer M-Pesa payments to the correct account.

When a consumer pays to the Simon Fresh Water Paybill (247247), Safaricom sends a callback to this portal. The dashboard shows:

  • Configuration — current environment (sandbox / production) and Paybill number.
  • Register URLs — one-time setup that tells Safaricom where to send payment notifications.
  • Recent Payments — every payment with status matched (auto-assigned to a consumer) or unmatched (needs manual assignment).

For unmatched payments, use the search box to find the correct consumer by name, phone, or meter number — then click Assign.

Auto-matching: Payments are matched to the consumer whose meter number equals the M-Pesa account reference, with a fallback match by phone number.
8 📢

Bulk SMS

Purpose: Send bill reminders to multiple consumers with overdue balances in one action.

  • By default, the list shows only OVERDUE and OVERDUE_APPROACHING consumers.
  • Tick Include DUE to also include recently-overdue consumers.
  • Select up to 30 consumers per batch (protects the SMS gateway).
  • Click Send SMS to Selected — each consumer receives a personalised message with their name, meter number, and outstanding amount.
Consent reminder: These messages go only to consumers who have agreed to receive billing notifications on their registered phone number.
9 📊

Monthly Reports

Purpose: Generate a formal monthly billing report for any past period.

  1. Select the Month and Year.
  2. The Preview table shows the 20 consumer rows that will appear in the PDF.
  3. Click 📊 Download Report (PDF) to save the report.

Each report contains: summary totals, total consumption, amount billed, amount paid, outstanding balance, and per-consumer breakdowns.

Period notes: If the selected period is in the future or has no recorded activity, the portal displays a polite message instead of an empty report.
10 ⚙️

Settings

Purpose: Manage your administrator account.

  • Change Username — must be unique across the system.
  • Change Reset Phone — the number that receives password-reset codes.
  • Change Password — requires your current password; you'll be signed out after changing it.
Security: After changing your password, all other active sessions end automatically — every device must sign in again with the new password.
11 📄

Legal Pages

At the bottom of every page, the footer contains two links:

  • Privacy Policy — how consumer data is collected, stored, shared, and protected (in compliance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019).
  • Terms of Use — the rules governing use of the portal, billing calculations, M-Pesa payments, and account termination.

These pages are publicly accessible from every page of the site.
